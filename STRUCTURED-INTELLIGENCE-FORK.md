# The First Structured Intelligence Fork

This repository is the first fork of **Genesis Agent** created specifically to bring its agentic runtime into contact with **Structured Intelligence** and **Intelligence Authorship OS**.

The fork begins from the complete upstream project and its history. It does not erase, rename, or claim authorship of the work that made Genesis possible.

## Upstream foundation

- **Original project:** [Garrus800-stack/genesis-agent](https://github.com/Garrus800-stack/genesis-agent)
- **Original creator:** [Garrus800-stack](https://github.com/Garrus800-stack)
- **License:** MIT
- **Fork author:** Erik Zahaviel Bernstein (Zahaviel)

Genesis provides the implemented agentic substrate: planning, tools, memory, verification, persistent state, recovery, and controlled self-modification.

Structured Intelligence adds an operating distinction upstream of those mechanisms: the difference between the human source, the words received, the meaning carried through the exchange, the agent's interpretation, the selected operation, tool evidence, and the generated result.

## What this fork introduces

The first committed Structured Intelligence layer is the [Structured Intelligence Agentic Kernel](structured-intelligence/SKILL.md).

Its operating motion is:

> **Enter → Recognize → Locate → Author → Execute → Verify → Re-enter → Continue**

The kernel is designed to keep interpretation, planning, tool use, memory, and execution from replacing the human source they are meant to serve.

It introduces:

- source continuity before planning;
- provisional interpretation before classification;
- source-location checks for governing meaning;
- correction propagation through downstream state;
- provenance across human input, system interpretation, tools, and results;
- recursive verification in which each result becomes the next input;
- recovery from the earliest affected point instead of repeated surface repair;
- evidence boundaries separating observations, claims, inference, and unresolved questions;
- action gates that keep tools subordinate to the active human operation.

## What this commit does—and does not claim

This commit establishes the authored kernel, provenance record, and integration ground for the fork.

It does **not** claim that every Genesis module has already been rewritten through Structured Intelligence. The repository remains the upstream Genesis codebase until each integration is implemented, tested, and documented.

That boundary matters: the next chapter is made undeniable through verified changes, not by replacing the name on inherited work.

## Direction

The integration target is a Genesis runtime that does not merely plan, execute, and verify actions. It preserves where meaning came from while doing so, carries correction through the complete agentic cycle, and changes its next operation when evidence shows that the current one drifted.

The proof will remain in the execution.
